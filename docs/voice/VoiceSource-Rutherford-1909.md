# VoiceSource · Ernest Rutherford（约 1908–1911 · Coupland）

**用途**：供「文案叙事」写 VoiceCard；**不是**游戏台词。  
**人物**：Ernest Rutherford · Manchester Coupland Street 物理楼语境  
**马斯登／盖革**：本轮默认**不出声**；文末仅附实验事实指针供旁白转述。  
**禁止**：编造日记口语；把 1930s「十五英寸炮弹」当 1909 暗室原话；虚构具名对话。

---

## 1. 可模仿语气轴（Voice axes）

1. **实验优先**：先讲观测与装置路径，再给结构假说；喜用 “experimental knowledge / persistent attack / yielded to the assault”。
2. **讲演腔（P0）**：条理、回顾式叙事、承认他人贡献（Becquerel、Bragg、Boltwood、Giesel…），偶有第一人称 “I independently…”／“Time does not allow me…”。
3. **自信但可撤回**：证据链够长才说 “little room for doubt”，同时保留 “decisive proof” 门槛。
4. **竞技／急切（书信，P1）**：对 Thomson 模型可更冲（须标书信出处）；实验室口语可借一点，但勿满嘴私信八卦。
5. **术语时代感**：优先 *α-particle / emanation / transformation / central charge / diffuse reflection*；**慎用**后世旗号 *nucleus*（1911 文主要说中心电荷）。
6. **归功实验室**：大角计数苦活归 Geiger／Marsden；自己定方向与理论框——**旁白可提，本轮二人不出声**。
7. **书面腔 ≠ 口语**：1911 *Phil. Mag.* 句法可借鉴严谨，勿整段当对话。

### A. 1908 Nobel Lecture — *The Chemical Nature of the Alpha Particles from Radioactive Substances*

**source_tier：B**（一手讲演）  
**citation**：E. Rutherford · Nobel Lecture, 11 Dec 1908 · https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/1908/rutherford/lecture/  
**register**：正式讲演／公开科学口语  
**本地**：`/workspace/kaozheng/reading-notes/voice/rutherford_1908_nobel.txt`（自官网 HTML 抽取；旧 PDF 链失效）

| # | quote | voice_use |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | “With increase of experimental knowledge there has been a growing recognition that a large part of radioactive phenomena is intimately connected with the expulsion of the α-particles.” | 开场定调：实验知识↑ → 重视 α |
| N2 | “…to trace the long and arduous path trodden by the experimenter in the attempts to solve the difficult question of the chemical nature of the α-particles.” | 自我定位＝实验者走长路；可学 “arduous path” |
| N3 | “α-rays were first observed in 1899… and during the last six years there has been a persistent attack on this great problem, which has finally yielded to the assault when the resources of the attack seemed almost exhausted.” | 军事隐喻式攻坚；坚持到资源将尽 |
| N4 | “On account of their insignificant penetrating power, the α-rays were at first considered of little importance…” | 承认早期低估；后发制人叙事 |
| N5 | “I independently arrived at the same conclusion from consideration of a variety of evidence.” | 第一人称、证据多样、不夸张发明权 |
| N6 | “This showed that the α-rays consisted of projected charged particles while the direction of deflection indicated that each particle carried a positive charge.” | 短句、观测→推论 |
| N7 | “From the independence of the rate of transformation of chemical and physical agencies, it was recognized that the transformation was atomic and not molecular in character.” | 原子性蜕变；课堂／讲演可复用 |
| N8 | “The proof that the α-particle was an ejected atom of matter at once strengthened this conclusion…” | “ejected atom of matter”——1909 暗室仍可用的物质图景 |
| N9 | “…I proved that the α-particles, whether expelled from radium, thorium or actinium, were identical in mass…” | 实验自述；跨放射源同一性 |
| N10 | “I suggested that, in all probability, the α-particle was a helium atom which carried two unit charges.” | 概率措辞 “in all probability”；双电荷氦 |
| N11 | “While the whole train of evidence… indicates with little room for doubt that the α-particle is a projected helium atom, there was still wanting a decisive and incontrovertible proof…” | **语气金句**：证据充分仍要 decisive proof |
| N12 | “…the α-particle, after its charge is neutralized, is a helium atom.”（计数实验语境） | 收束定义；可作实验室解释腔 |

**era_caution**：诺奖在斯德哥尔摩讲演，比 Coupland 暗室更正式；可降正式度，勿改成俚语。

## 5. 给文案的最短用法

1. VoiceCard 主声线锚在 **N1–N12 + 语气轴**。  
2. 放射／α 科普腔可掺 **T2–T4**。  
3. 对 Thomson 急切感只用 **L1**（标转引）。  
4. 论证核／中心电荷结构时，模仿 **P1 书面逻辑**，改写成短句口语，勿照搬长句。  
5. 本轮不写 Marsden／Geiger VoiceSource。
